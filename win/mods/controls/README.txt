Controls v0.1 (Modernization Core)

Default keyboard and mouse layout: WASD move, mouse look, left click or F = attack / talk / open / confirm, E or Space or Ctrl = lock on (and Back in menus),
right click = guard, G = use the active item, Tab = menu, Esc or Enter = pause, Q camera behind you, R first-person look, Z next target,
1-3 = choose and use quick item 1-3, 4-9 = switch to party member 1-6 (Toan, Xiao, Goro, Ruby, Ungaga, Osmond), mouse wheel = choose the active item
in a dungeon / move through menus.

Remap any action in the game: open the Hub (K, or L1+R1+R3) > Controls. Cross (F / left click) replaces the keys of the highlighted action, Square (G)
adds a second key, "Reset all" restores the defaults. Choices are kept in mods/_bindings.json.

First person: V toggles it in dungeons and towns. The mouse looks around; look down to see your hands, feet and weapon.
Autosave: see the autosave mod (top slot of the save list).
Towns: 4-9 play as that party member (once they have joined), Backspace (select) cycles through the party.
