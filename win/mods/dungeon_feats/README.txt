Dungeon Feats v0.1
Floor cleared (all enemies defeated): gilda bonus that grows with floor and dungeon (idea from DarkCloud-Expanded, which re-enabled the game's unused floor clear message).
Kill streaks: kills within 8 seconds of each other; bonuses at x5, x10 and every 20.
Bestiary: first kill of each enemy kind is logged; milestones at 10, 50 and 150 kills pay gilda.
Trophies: 11 one-time rewards for kills, streaks, floors cleared and kinds of enemy met.
Enemy names: from DarkCloud-Expanded data (BSD-2-Clause, license in LICENSE-DarkCloud-Expanded.txt).
All progress saves per slot (mods/_store/dungeon_feats). Nothing here changes combat.
