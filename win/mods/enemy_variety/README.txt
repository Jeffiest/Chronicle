Enemy Variety v0.1 - after DarkCloud-Expanded's faster / randomized enemies and "every enemy can revive" options
(https://github.com/Tororoi/DarkCloud-Expanded, BSD-2-Clause).

- Each enemy gets its own random speed (movement and animation), so a group is no longer uniform.
- Any ordinary enemy has a chance (25%) to survive a killing blow once: it drops to 30% life, is stunned for a moment, then fights on.
  Bosses and key-carrying enemies are exempt.
Values are on the Mod Settings screen (F1) under "Variety". Needs the engine function dc.set_monster_speed (this build).
