Expanded Abilities v0.2 - weapon effects inspired by DarkCloud-Expanded (https://github.com/Tororoi/DarkCloud-Expanded, BSD-2-Clause; license in LICENSE-DarkCloud-Expanded.txt).

Cross Hinder: x2 damage against the undead (their Sanctifier; the "undead never revive" part is not included).
Mobius Ring: +2.5% damage per second of an unbroken fight, up to +60% (an approximation of their charge-based effect).
Brave Ark: each hit cures freeze, poison, curse and goo on the wielder.
Heaven's Cloud: 50% chance to goo (slow) the enemy on a hit. Snail: 5%. Frozen Tuna: 5% chance to stop a non-ice target.
Cactus: absorbs one drop of water per 100 damage dealt.
Aga's Sword: 15 less damage taken (their +15 defense).
Not included: effects that need new visuals, clones, charges or weapon-generation hooks (Sun Sword's flash, Big Bang's explosions,
Mirage's decoy, Angel Gear's reflector, Maneater's curse, Macho Sword's ABS overflow, Kitchen Knife's springs, the "Fragile"/"Drain" generation rolls).
