Floor Bounty v0.1. The first enemy you meet on a floor sets that floor's bounty: kill 6 of the same kind (rarer kinds are worth more
the deeper you are) and you are paid gilda, scaled by dungeon and floor. The counter shows under the top of the screen. Names come from
Dungeon Feats' list (DarkCloud-Expanded data, BSD-2-Clause). Reward size can be changed on the Mod Settings screen.
