Floor Mutators v0.1
Each floor (from the second) has a 35% chance, a little higher in deeper dungeons, of one modifier, announced with a banner and shown at the top of the screen:
Glass Cannon (you deal and take +50%), Iron Hide (enemies take -25%, pay double gilda), Bounty (2.5x gilda), Fountain (water refills), Scorched (water drains faster), Lucky Day (15% of hits deal double).
Edit CHANCE and the list at the top of scripts/main.lua.
